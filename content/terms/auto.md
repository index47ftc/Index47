---
title: Autonomous
Aliases: [auto]
---

**Autonomous** in the context of FTC is known as the first 30 seconds of each match that is fully automated. This means that drivers are not allowed, and the robot has to move and complete autonomous-designated tasks during this period which are pre-programmed. Despite vast changes that occur during the season game, autonomous is always stable as the first 30 seconds of the season and normally holds a task that is unique to it and different from teleop, however the DECODE and Into the Deep seasons defy this.

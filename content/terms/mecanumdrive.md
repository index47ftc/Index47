---
title: Mecanum Drive
aliases: [Mecanum, Mec]
---

**Mecanum drive** is a drivetrain type that has 4 [**mecanum wheels**(https://www.gobilda.com/gripforce-mecanum-wheel-set-o104mm-40a-durometer-rollers/). A mecanum wheel has rollers that are oriented 45º, allowing for omnidirectional movement depending on the direction the wheels spin. Mecanum drives are very easy to make and simple to program, however, they are slower than swerve drives and are lack speed and torque when diagonally straffing.

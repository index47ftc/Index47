---
title: Shooting Outside of the Zone 
aliases: [SOTZ]
---

**Shooting Outside of the Zone** is a phrase used in the FTC community, popularized in the DECODE season. It essentially means that a robot has shot artifacts without being at least touching one of the white zone lines, which causes many penalties. It is often seen with robots that are capable of [SOTM](https://index47.org/sotm).

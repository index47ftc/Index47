FTCodeSim (formerly KinetX) is a FTC physics motor control simulator developed by Jacob Ophoven!
It uses AdvantageScope logs to show simulations of algorithms such as predictive braking, bang-bang, PID, SquID, and PIDFs without a robot. Check the [repository](https://github.com/jjophoven/FTCodeSim) for more details

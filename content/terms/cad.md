title: Computer Aided Design
aliases: [CAD]

**Computer Aided Design** is an online tool that students in FTC often use to design their robot, and subsystems. Traditionally, a platform known as Onshape is used for this. Common parts like goBilda and REV can be replicated to fit into this software making an accurate depiction of your robot throughout the season. To get started with this open [**onshape.com**](https://onshape.com) or visit **Onshape** on the search bar

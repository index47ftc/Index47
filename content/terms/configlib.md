ConfigLib is a configurable and simplified [OnShape](https://index47.org/onshape) parts library 
(focused on goBilda) made by Simba, and recently with several more FTC students spread across various teams.
ConfigLib accepts open contributions, with many of their open sourced OP configurations being submitted. 
Administrators for ConfigLib needed with urgency can be contacted through their [discord](https://discord.gg/YN6KCaW6tv) community

---
title: Multinational Tech Invitational
aliases: [MTI]
---

The **Multinational Tech Invitational**, know as the Maryland Tech Invitational prior to the 2025-26 DECODE competition, is a highly selection robot-based off-season event usually held at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory. To apply, teams are usually required to submit a high scoring solo video. There are only 3 awards, different from the usual FTC awards (Mechatronics Synergy, Hardware Mastery, Software Innovation and Quality). Visit [firstchesapeake.org/mti](https://www.firstchesapeake.org/mti) for more information.

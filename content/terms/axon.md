---
title: Axon Robotics
---

**Axon Robotics** is an FTC parts supplier known for their high-performance programmable servos. Unlike traditional servos from goBilda and REV, axon servos can offer full customizability of sensitivity and have an easily accessable swerve mode to those who choose. It has 3 forms, Axon MICRO for small powered movements and not a lot of torque, Axon MINI for normal movements, and Axon MAX which the most powerful item. Visit [axon-robotics.com](https://axon-robotics.com/) to look at these products in depth.

---
title: Kiwi Drive
aliases: [Kiwi]
---

**Kiwi drive** is a drivetrain type that has 3 [**omni wheels**](https://www.gobilda.com/omni-wheels) oriented in a triangle. Advantages of kiwi drives are that they are extremely agile and have very fast acceleration. However, they only have 3 wheel so they lack torque.

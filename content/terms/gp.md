---
title: Gracious Professionalism
aliases: [GP]
---

**Gracious Professionalism** is the core value of [FIRST](https://www.firstinspires.org/) that encourages high-quality work, respect for others, and valuing everyone's contributions while still maintaining a professional conduct. High levels of gracious professionalism can earn awards such as the Inspire Award which earns 60 ranking points! Gracious professionalism lies outside of competition, and can be applied to private team meetings and social media related to FIRST.

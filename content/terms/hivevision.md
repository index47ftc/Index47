title: Hive Vision
aliases: [hive vision, hive-vision]
---

**Hive Vision** is a computer-vision pipeline that detects the season's **game elements** (Pollen and
Nectar) in real time, running an SSD detector on a **Limelight 3A**. It also includes the
[Ball Chase API](https://index47.org/ballchase) for tracking, locking, and pickup without odometry.
Source and docs: https://github.com/sidhuharjas/Hive-Vision

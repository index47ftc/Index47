---
title: Robot in 3 days
aliases: [RI3D]
---

**Robot in 3 days** is a concept for FTC and FRC teams to challenge themselves the **instant** a season is released, the challenge is to complete a basic robot in **3 days** after the season comes out, in attempt to have as many game aspects somewhat **solved** in that time. This can be applied to different challenges such as **Robot in 30 hours** which is very common as well.

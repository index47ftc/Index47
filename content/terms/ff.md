---
title: Feedforward
aliases: [FF]
---

**Feedforward** is a PID system used to determine how much power it needs to reach the **target**, which is traditionally used along side with **PID**, and contain values such as **kS**, **kV**, **kA**, and many more depending on the subsystem. Visit [ctrlaltftc.com](https://www.ctrlaltftc.com/feedforward-control) for more information on what FF is.

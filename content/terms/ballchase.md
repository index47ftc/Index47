**Ball Chase API**, part of the **Hive Vision** project, is an FTC library for chasing and picking up
**game elements** using a **Limelight 3A** on mecanum or **Pedro** drivetrains. It provides a
tracker with target lock and multi-frame confirmation, a no-odometry chase state machine, and
one-line autos ([BallHunt](https://github.com/sidhuharjas/Hive-Vision/blob/main/ftc_ball_chase_lib/wrapper/BallHunt.java)).
Source and docs: https://github.com/sidhuharjas/Hive-Vision
